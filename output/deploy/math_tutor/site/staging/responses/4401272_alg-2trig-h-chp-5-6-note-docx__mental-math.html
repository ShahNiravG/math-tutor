Law of Cosines and Heron’s Formula – Mental Math Questions

1. True or False: The Law of Cosines reduces to the Pythagorean Theorem when the included angle is 90°.

2. If the sides of a triangle are 3, 4, and 5 and the angle opposite the side of length 5 is 90°, what type of triangle is it?

3. In triangle ABC, if side \( a = 10 \), \( b = 10 \), and the angle between them is 60°, is the triangle equilateral, isosceles, or scalene?

4. Which side of a triangle (a, b, or c) will be the longest if the angle opposite to it is the largest?

5. The Law of Cosines calculates an angle when you know all three sides. What information do you need to use the Law of Cosines to find a missing side?

6. If the semiperimeter of a triangle is 10, and the sides are 3, 4, and 5, what is the perimeter?

7. Bearings are measured from which reference directions according to the navigation section? (a) East-West (b) North-South (c) Any direction

8. Yes or No: For triangle sides a = 5, b = 5, c = 5, does Heron’s formula simplify the calculation of area compared to \(A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C\)?

9. If two sides of a triangle are 7 and 7, and the included angle is 180°, what is the length of the third side?

10. True or False: The Law of Cosines can be used for right, obtuse, and acute triangles alike.
