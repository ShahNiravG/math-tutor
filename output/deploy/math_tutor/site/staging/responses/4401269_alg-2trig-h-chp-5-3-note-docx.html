2. Core Definitions, Theorems, and Formulas

A. Definitions

  • Standard Position: An angle with its vertex at the origin and initial side along the positive x-axis.
  • Reference Angle: The acute angle formed by the terminal side of θ and the x-axis.
  • Quadrantal Angle: An angle whose terminal side lies on the x- or y-axis.

B. Trigonometric Functions (using point P(x, y), distance \( r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \))

  • \( \sin \theta = \frac{y}{r} \)
  • \( \cos \theta = \frac{x}{r} \)
  • \( \tan \theta = \frac{y}{x} \) \((x \neq 0)\)
  • \( \csc \theta = \frac{r}{y} \) \((y \neq 0)\)
  • \( \sec \theta = \frac{r}{x} \) \((x \neq 0)\)
  • \( \cot \theta = \frac{x}{y} \) \((y \neq 0)\)

C. Signs of Trigonometric Functions by Quadrant

| Quadrant | Positive Functions | Negative Functions | |----------|--------------------|------------------------| | I | all | none | | II | sin, csc | cos, sec, tan, cot | | III | tan, cot | sin, csc, cos, sec | | IV | cos, sec | sin, csc, tan, cot |

D. Trigonometric Identities

Reciprocal Identities

  • \( \csc \theta = \frac{1}{\sin \theta} \)
  • \( \sec \theta = \frac{1}{\cos \theta} \)
  • \( \cot \theta = \frac{1}{\tan \theta} \)
  • \( \tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \)
  • \( \cot \theta = \frac{\cos \theta}{\sin \theta} \)

Pythagorean Identities

  • \( \sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1 \)
  • \( \tan^2 \theta + 1 = \sec^2 \theta \)
  • \( 1 + \cot^2 \theta = \csc^2 \theta \)

E. Triangle Area Using Trig

  • For triangle with sides \( a \), \( b \) and included angle \( \theta \):

\( A = \frac{1}{2} ab \sin \theta \)

3. Worked Study Guide

A. Finding Trig Functions at Any Angle

1. Find the reference angle: Reduce θ to an angle between 0° and 90° with the x-axis. 2. Determine the sign: Use the quadrant to find the sign. 3. Calculate value: Use reference angle and sign.

B. Example:

Find \( \csc 135^\circ \):

  • Reference angle = \( 180^\circ - 135^\circ = 45^\circ \)
  • 135° is in Quadrant II, sine is positive.
  • \( \sin 45^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \), so \( \csc 45^\circ = \frac{1}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = \sqrt{2} \)
  • Answer: \( \csc 135^\circ = \sqrt{2} \)

C. Applying Trigonometric Identities

If \( \sin \theta = \frac{3}{5} \), \( \theta \) in Quadrant II:

  • \( \cos \theta = -\sqrt{1 - (\sin \theta)^2} = -\sqrt{1 - \frac{9}{25}} = -\sqrt{\frac{16}{25}} = -\frac{4}{5} \)

D. Area of Triangle Example

Given \( a = 10 \), \( b = 22 \), \( \theta = 10^\circ \):

  • \( A = \frac{1}{2}1022\sin 10^\circ \approx 110\sin 10^\circ \approx 110*0.1736 \approx 19.10 \)

4. Practice Problems (with Answers)

Practice 1

Find the reference angle for \( -199^\circ \)

  • \( 199^\circ \) is \( 19^\circ \) past 180°, so reference = 199° – 180° = 19°.
  • Since it’s negative, the reference angle is 19° past -180°, so reference = 19°.

Practice 2

Evaluate \( \tan \left( -\frac{4\pi}{3} \right) \)

  • \( -\frac{4\pi}{3} + 2\pi = \frac{2\pi}{3} \), reference angle = \( \frac{\pi}{3} \).
  • \( \tan\left( -\frac{4\pi}{3} \right) = \tan\left( \frac{2\pi}{3} \right) = -\sqrt{3} \)

Practice 3

Which quadrant if \( \tan \theta < 0 \) and \( \sin \theta < 0 \)?

  • Tan negative: II or IV. Sin negative: III or IV. So Quadrant IV.

Practice 4

Find area: triangle sides 8, 13, included angle 42°.

  • \( A = \frac{1}{2}813\sin42^\circ \approx 52\sin42^\circ \approx 52*0.6691 \approx 34.79 \)

Practice 5

If \( \sin \theta = \frac{3}{10} \), \( \sec \theta > 0 \), find \( \cos \theta \)

  • \( \cos \theta = \sqrt{1-(\frac{3}{10})^2} = \sqrt{1-\frac{9}{100}} = \sqrt{\frac{91}{100}} = \frac{\sqrt{91}}{10} \)
  • Since \( \sec \theta > 0 \), \( \cos \theta > 0 \).
