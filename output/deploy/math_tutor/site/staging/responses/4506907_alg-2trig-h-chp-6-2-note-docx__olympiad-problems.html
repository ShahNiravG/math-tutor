Problems

1. Without using a calculator, evaluate \(\cos\left(225^\circ\right)+\sin\left(495^\circ\right)\). Quickly justify your reasoning and sign choices.

2. If \(\tan t = -\frac{3}{4}\) and \(t\) is in Quadrant II, find the exact value of \(\sin t + \cos t\).

3. Find all \(t\) in \(0 < t < 2\pi\) such that \(\sec t < 0\) and \(\cot t > 0\). Express your answer in interval form.

4. Let \(y = 7 \sin x + 24 \cos x\). What is the maximum value of \(y\) as \(x\) varies over all real numbers? (No calculus.)

5. Suppose \(\sin t + \cos t = 1\). Determine the value of \(\sin^4 t + \cos^4 t\).

6. Evaluate \(\tan(13\pi) + \cot(17\pi)\) mentally, using periodicity and symmetry.
