Certainly! Here are 10 mental math questions based on the main concepts from your Algebra 2/Trig material on trigonometric functions of real numbers. These are meant to be answerable without paper and pencil and test conceptual understanding, not detailed calculations.

1. Unit Circle Coordinates

If a point on the unit circle has an x-coordinate of 1/2, what is the cosine of the corresponding angle?

2. Quadrant Signs

Which trigonometric functions are positive in Quadrant III?

3. Function Domains

Is \(\tan(\frac{\pi}{2})\) defined? Why or why not?

4. Reciprocal Functions

If \(\sin t = \frac{1}{2}\), what is \(\csc t\)?

5. Quadrant Identification

If \(\sin t < 0\) and \(\cos t > 0\), in which quadrant does terminal side of \(t\) lie?

6. Even-Odd Property

Is the cosine function even or odd?

7. Function Signs

If a point is in Quadrant II, is \(\sec t\) positive or negative?

8. Pythagorean Identity

If \(\sin^2 t = \frac{1}{4}\), what is \(\cos^2 t\)?

9. Reference Angle

What is the reference angle for \(210^\circ\) (or \(\frac{7\pi}{6}\))?

10. Function Value through Symmetry

If \(\sin(\alpha) = 0.6\), what is \(\sin(-\alpha)\)?
