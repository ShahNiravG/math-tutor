Certainly! Here are 10 mental math questions designed to target key concepts from the PDF, all answerable without pen and paper.

1. Concepts: Center of Circle

What do you call the fixed point from which every point on a circle is equidistant?

2. Circle Equation

If a circle is centered at the origin with radius 4, what is its equation?

3. Parabola Shape

Does the graph of \( y = 2x^2 + 3x + 1 \) open upwards or downwards?

4. Axis of Symmetry for Parabola

If a parabola’s vertex is at the origin and the equation is \( x^2 = 8y \), what axis is the parabola symmetric about?

5. Parabola Direction

For the equation \( y^2 = -12x \), does the parabola open to the left or the right?

6. Focus Location

For a parabola with equation \( x^2 = 4py \) and vertex at (0,0), what is the y-coordinate of its focus?

7. Directrix Equation

If a standard vertical parabola has focus (0,5), what is the equation of its directrix?

8. Recognizing Parabola Orientation

True or False: The parabola \( y^2 = 16x \) has its axis parallel to the x-axis.

9. "Width" of Parabola

What is the length of the latus rectum (focal diameter) of the parabola \( x^2 = 12y \)?

10. Opening of Parabola

If \( p < 0 \) in the equation \( y^2 = 4px \), in which direction does the parabola open?
