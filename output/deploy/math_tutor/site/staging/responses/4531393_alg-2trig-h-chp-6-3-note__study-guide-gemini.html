2. Core Definitions, Theorems, and Formulas

Definitions:

  • Periodic Function: A function $f$ is periodic if there is a positive number $p$ such that $f(t + p) = f(t)$ for every $t$.
  • Period: The least such positive number $p$ (if it exists) is the period of $f$. It represents the length of one complete cycle of the graph.
  • Amplitude: The number $|a|$, which represents the largest value these functions attain from their midline.

Core Formulas & Transformations: For the general shifted sine and cosine curves (where $k > 0$): $$y = c + a \sin k(x - b)$$ $$y = c + a \cos k(x - b)$$

  • Amplitude: $|a|$
  • Period: $\frac{2\pi}{k}$
  • Vertical Shift: $c$
  • Horizontal Shift: $b$
  • Graphing Interval: To graph one complete period, use the interval $[b, b + \frac{2\pi}{k}]$.
  • Horizontal Stretching/Shrinking: The value of $k$ shrinks the graph horizontally if $k > 1$, and stretches the graph horizontally if $k < 1$.

The 5-Point Sinusoidal Patterns: (Where M = Middle, H = High, L = Low)

  • $+\cos t$ = H M L M H
  • $-\cos t$ = L M H M L
  • $+\sin t$ = M H M L M
  • $-\sin t$ = M L M H M

3. Worked Study Guide: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Understanding the Base Graphs Before shifting anything, you must know what basic sine and cosine waves look like. Both have a standard period of $2\pi$.

  • Sine starts at the midline (0), goes to its highest point (1), back to the midline (0), down to its lowest point (-1), and back to the midline (0). Pattern: M H M L M.
  • Cosine starts at its highest point (1), goes to the midline (0), down to its lowest point (-1), back to the midline (0), and up to its highest point (1). Pattern: H M L M H.

Step 2: Identifying the Transformations When you look at an equation like $y = 3 + 2\sin 4(x - \frac{\pi}{2})$, identify your $a, b, c,$ and $k$ values based on the formula $y = c + a \sin k(x - b)$:

  • $c = 3$ (Vertical Shift): The whole graph moves up by 3. Your "Middle" (M) line is now $y=3$ instead of the x-axis.
  • $a = 2$ (Amplitude): The graph stretches vertically. From your midline of 3, your "High" (H) points will be $3+2=5$, and your "Low" (L) points will be $3-2=1$.
  • $k = 4$ (Used for Period): The period is $\frac{2\pi}{k}$. Here, $\frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2}$. The graph completes one full wave in $\frac{\pi}{2}$ units along the x-axis. Because $k > 1$, this graph is horizontally shrunk.
  • $b = \frac{\pi}{2}$ (Horizontal Shift): The graph shifts to the right. Instead of starting at $x=0$, your first point starts at $x=\frac{\pi}{2}$.

Step 3: Sketching One Complete Period To sketch the graph accurately, you need a starting x-value and an ending x-value. The document provides the interval formula: $[b, b + \frac{2\pi}{k}]$. Using our example above:

  • Start at $b = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
  • End at $b + \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} = \pi$.
  • Your 5 sinusoidal points (M H M L M for positive sine) will be evenly spaced between $x = \frac{\pi}{2}$ and $x = \pi$.

4. Practice Problems

Problem 1: Identify the amplitude, period, vertical shift, and horizontal shift for the function: $y = -4 + 5\cos 2(x - \pi)$

Problem 2: State the appropriate mathematical interval $[b, b + \frac{2\pi}{k}]$ on which to graph one complete period of the function: $y = \sin 3(x + \frac{\pi}{6})$

Problem 3: A tuning fork's vibrations are modeled by the function $v(t) = 0.8\sin(660\pi t)$. Based on the formulas in the text, what is the period of this vibration?

Problem 4: If you are sketching the graph of $y = -3\sin(x)$, what 5-point sequence (using M, H, and L) should you use, and why?

Problem 5: Does a $k$ value of $\frac{1}{2}$ stretch or shrink the graph horizontally compared to the base graph?

Answers to Practice Problems

  • Answer 1: Amplitude = $5$ (from $|5|$); Period = $\pi$ (from $\frac{2\pi}{2}$); Vertical shift = $-4$; Horizontal shift = $\pi$.
  • Answer 2: First, identify $b = -\frac{\pi}{6}$ and $k = 3$. The period is $\frac{2\pi}{3}$. The interval is $[-\frac{\pi}{6}, -\frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{2\pi}{3}]$. By getting common denominators ($-\frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{4\pi}{6}$), the final interval is $[-\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{2}]$.
  • Answer 3: The $k$ value is $660\pi$. The period formula is $\frac{2\pi}{k}$. Therefore, the period is $\frac{2\pi}{660\pi} = \frac{1}{330}$ seconds.
  • Answer 4: You would use M L M H M. You use this because it is a negative sine function, which reflects the standard positive sine wave upside down.
  • Answer 5: Because $k = \frac{1}{2}$, which is less than 1 ($k < 1$), it stretches the graph horizontally.
