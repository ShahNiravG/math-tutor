Problems

1. Evaluate \(\cos^{-1}(\sin(\sin^{-1}\frac{3}{5}))\) without a calculator, expressing your answer in radians.

2. If \(\tan^{-1}(x) + \tan^{-1}(y) = \frac{\pi}{4}\), what is the value of \(x + y + xy\)?

3. Simplify \(\sin(\cos^{-1}(x)) + \cos(\sin^{-1}(x))\) for \(x \in [-1,1]\).

4. Without evaluating directly, decide if the following two are equal for \(x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]\): \(\arccos(\sin x)\) and \(\arcsin(\cos x)\).

5. Find the exact value of \(\tan(\arccos(\frac{1}{3}))\) in simplest radical form.

6. Let \(A = \arctan 2 + \arctan 3\). Find the exact value of \(\tan A\).
