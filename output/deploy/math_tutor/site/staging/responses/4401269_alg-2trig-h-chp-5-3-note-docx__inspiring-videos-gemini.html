2. All 6 Trig Functions on the Unit Circle

Creator: Schattman Math URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dsf6ADwJ66E Why it inspires: This beautifully animated video unifies all six trigonometric functions into a single, cohesive geometric structure on the unit circle. Instead of making students memorize the Pythagorean identities algebraically, it visually 'proves' them using interactive geometry, sparking curiosity about how deeply interconnected math can be.
