Problems

1. A triangle has sides \(13,14,15\). Find the largest angle.

2. Two sides of a triangle are \(20\) and \(21\), and the included angle is \(60^\circ\). Find the third side, then the area.

3. A triangle has sides \(25,25,30\). Find its area without using a calculator.

4. A hiker walks \(12\) km due east, then \(5\sqrt2\) km on a bearing \(N45^\circ E\). How far is the hiker from the starting point?

5. Point \(B\) is \(100\) miles from point \(A\) at bearing \(N60^\circ E\). A pilot mistakenly flies due east from \(A\) for \(50\) miles. How far is the pilot from \(B\)?

6. A triangle has sides \(29,29,42\). Find the exact value of \(\cos\theta\), where \(\theta\) is one of the base angles.
