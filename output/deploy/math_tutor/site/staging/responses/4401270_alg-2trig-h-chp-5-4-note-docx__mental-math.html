Certainly! Here are 10 mental math questions that test the core concepts from the provided PDF on inverse trigonometric functions and right triangles. They are designed to be answerable without a calculator, paper, or pencil, focusing on properties, domains, basic values, and definitions:

1. Domain & Range Understanding

Q1: What is the domain of the inverse sine function, \(\sin^{-1}x\)?

  • A: \([-1, 1]\)

Q2: What is the range of the inverse cosine function, \(\cos^{-1}x\)?

  • A: \([0, \pi]\)

2. Standard Values

Q3: What is \(\sin^{-1}(0)\) in radians?

  • A: \(0\)

Q4: What is \(\cos^{-1}(1)\) in radians?

  • A: \(0\)

Q5: What is \(\tan^{-1}(1)\) in radians?

  • A: \(\frac{\pi}{4}\)

3. Connections & Properties

Q6: True or False: \(\sin^{-1}(-x) = -\sin^{-1}(x)\) for every \(x\) in the domain.

  • A: True

Q7: If \(\theta = \arccos(0)\), what is \(\theta\) in radians?

  • A: \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)

4. Right Triangle Reasoning

Q8: If the opposite and adjacent sides of a right triangle are equal, what is \(\arctan(1)\) in degrees?

  • A: \(45^\circ\)

Q9: If \(\sin \theta = 1\), what is \(\theta\) in radians (principal value for inverse sine)?

  • A: \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)

5. Composite Function Intuition

Q10: If \(x\) is in the range \([-1,1]\), and you calculate \(\sin(\sin^{-1}(x))\), what do you get?

  • A: \(x\)
