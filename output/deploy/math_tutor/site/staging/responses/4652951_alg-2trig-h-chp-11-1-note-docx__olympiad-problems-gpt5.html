Problems

1. A circle centered at the origin is tangent to the parabola \(y^2=8x\). Find the radius of the circle.

2. The parabola \(x^2=4py\) and the circle \(x^2+(y-3)^2=9\) intersect in exactly one point. Find \(p\).

3. A point \(P\) lies on the parabola \(y^2=12x\). The distance from \(P\) to the focus equals \(10\). Find the coordinates of \(P\).

4. Two parabolas share the same vertex at the origin: \(x^2=4ay\) and \(y^2=4ax\), where \(a>0\). Find the area enclosed by their arcs.

5. A circle centered at \((2,0)\) passes through the focus of the parabola \(y^2=8x\) and is tangent to its directrix. Find the equation of the circle.

6. Let \(P\) be a point on the parabola \(x^2=4y\). A line through \(P\) with slope \(1\) meets the directrix at \(A\) and the \(y\)-axis at \(B\). Find the value of \(AB\), independent of the choice of \(P\).
