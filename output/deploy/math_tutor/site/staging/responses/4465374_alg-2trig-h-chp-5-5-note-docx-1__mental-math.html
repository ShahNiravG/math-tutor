Here are 10 mental math questions based on the Law of Sines concepts in the PDF. All questions are designed to be answerable without paper or calculator, focusing on understanding rather than calculation.

1. Core Concept

Q: In any triangle, what are the sides proportional to? > A: The sines of their opposite angles.

2. Formula Recall

Q: State the Law of Sines in the form relating sides and sines of angles. > A: \(\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\)

3. Triangle Cases

Q: If you know two angles and one side of a triangle (ASA or SAA), which Law would you use to solve for unknowns? > A: The Law of Sines.

4. Ambiguous Case (SSA)

Q: In the ambiguous (SSA) case, what must you compare the given side to first to check if a triangle is possible? > A: Compare it to the height \(h = b \sin A\).

5. No Solution Test

Q: In an SSA triangle where side \(a < h\), how many triangles exist? > A: No solution (no triangle).

6. Angle Calculation

Q: The sum of the angles in any triangle is always what? > A: 180°.

7. Double Solution

Q: What is one sign that two triangles are possible in the SSA case? > A: Both the angle and its supplement less than 180° lead to valid triangles.

8. Right Triangle Check

Q: If one of the angles in a triangle is 90°, what type of triangle is it? > A: Right triangle (not oblique).

9. More Than One Triangle?

Q: If you know two sides and an angle not between them (SSA), could you always get exactly one triangle? > A: No, you could get 0, 1, or 2 triangles.

10. Formula Comparison

Q: Does the Law of Sines work for all triangles, or only for right triangles? > A: Works for all triangles (not just right triangles).
