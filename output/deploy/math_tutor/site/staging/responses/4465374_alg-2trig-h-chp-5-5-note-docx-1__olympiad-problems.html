Problems

1. In triangle ABC, angle A is twice angle B and side a is twice side b. What is the value of angle C?

2. In triangle PQR, PQ = 5, QR = 7, and angle Q = 60°. Without using a calculator, determine whether two distinct triangles can be constructed with these parameters. Justify mentally.

3. Let triangle XYZ have sides x = 8, y = 15, and angle X = 30°. Show mentally whether a triangle exists, and if so, whether more than one triangle is possible (the ambiguous case).

4. Triangle DEF has angles D, E, F such that sin D + sin E = sin F. If sides d, e, f are opposite these angles respectively, what can you say about the ratios d : e : f?

5. In triangle LMN, LM = 10, MN = 7, and angle N = 90°. Find sin L + sin M using only properties of sines in a triangle.

6. Triangle ABC has sides a, b, c opposite angles A, B, C respectively, with a = √3, b = 1, and angle C = 120°. Find, without explicit calculation, whether triangle ABC is isosceles, scalene, or impossible.
