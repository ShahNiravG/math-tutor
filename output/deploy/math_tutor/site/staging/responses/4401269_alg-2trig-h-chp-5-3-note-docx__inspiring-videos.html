Based on your curriculum, the central concepts are trigonometric functions of angles (especially in the coordinate plane), signs/quadrants, reference angles, trigonometric identities, and areas involving triangles and trig. Here are two exceptional videos that inspire curiosity and provide visual, conceptual understanding—ideal for a motivated 14-year-old.

1. "How the Unit Circle Defines Trigonometric Functions" by 3Blue1Brown

Google Search: How the Unit Circle Defines Trigonometric Functions 3Blue1Brown YouTube

Explanation: This video introduces trigonometric functions using vivid animations and the geometry of the unit circle. It elegantly shows how sine, cosine, and tangent relate to coordinates on the circle as an angle sweeps through all quadrants. The visual intuition helps students grasp why trig functions change sign by quadrant and deeply understand reference angles. 3Blue1Brown is known for making abstract math concepts both beautiful and accessible.

2. "Why Sine, Cosine, and Tangent?" by Mathologer

Google Search: Why Sine, Cosine, and Tangent Mathologer YouTube

Explanation: Mathologer explores intuitively why these trigonometric functions are defined as they are, connecting triangles, the coordinate plane, and the unit circle. The video is packed with geometric insights and clever animations, helping students see the connections between right triangles, circles, and trig identities. It’s perfect for conceptual clarity and addresses the “big picture”—not just how, but why.

Why these? Both videos cover your Chapter 5.3’s heart: trig functions as geometric objects (not just rules), how their signs/quadrants work, and how geometry leads to algebraic identities. They also inspire curiosity by connecting trigonometry to real-world shapes and explorations, rather than focusing on rote procedure or competitive math.
