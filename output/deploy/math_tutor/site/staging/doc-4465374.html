What this chapter is about

This document is an instructional lesson on the Law of Sines, a fundamental trigonometric rule for solving oblique (non-right) triangles. It covers when and how to use the Law of Sines, especially in cases where triangles are not right-angled. The guide details the four types of triangle cases, placing particular emphasis on ambiguous SSA (Side-Side-Angle) triangles, and provides strategies for determining the number of possible triangle solutions (none, one, or two). It includes several example problems and word problems applying the Law of Sines.
